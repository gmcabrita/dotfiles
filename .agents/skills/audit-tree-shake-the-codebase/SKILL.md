Tree shake the codebase — all of it; along the lines of the ML ethos of "make impossible states unrepresentable". Propose simplifications and follow them through to their fullest conclusion as a result. Lexi Lambda's "Parse don't validate" is a guiding force here as well as Gary Bernhardt's "Functional Core, Imperative Shell" principle. The goal is to make the codebase as simple and maintainable as possible, removing unnecessary complexity and ensuring that the remaining code is clear, concise, and easy to understand.
